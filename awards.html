The Awards

Each year the Mustang Awards close out the season by recognizing SMU student-athletes for what they've achieved in competition, in the classroom and in the community. These are the honors presented on the night.

  • Highest Team GPA

    Recognizes one men's and one women's program for earning the highest team grade point average across the spring and fall semesters.

  • Student-Athlete Choice Award

    Chosen by fellow student-athletes for the teammate they see as a true role model in the community.

  • Individual and Team Community Award

    Honors the student-athlete and the team whose service has made a lasting difference across the SMU and Dallas communities.

  • Perseverance Award

    For student-athletes who showed remarkable determination in overcoming personal, medical or academic setbacks while staying in good academic standing.

  • Sportsmanship Award

    Celebrates student-athletes who compete with class, respect and integrity every time they step into the arena.

  • Red & Blue Award

    For the student-athletes with the most passion and spirit for SMU Athletics, who go above and beyond to support fellow athletes and the department.

  • Newcomer of the Year

    Recognizes first-year transfer student-athletes who made an immediate impact in their sport.

  • Freshman of the Year

    Honors first-year student-athletes whose arrival on the Hilltop made an immediate difference for their team.

  • Student-Athlete of the Year

    For team leaders who reached a high level of competitive success, kept up their academics, and lifted their teammates while representing SMU well.

THE SIGNATURE HONOR

Athletic Director's R.U.N. Award

Presented by the SMU Athletic Director to one male and one female student-athlete who embody the drive and determination of a Mustang.

R Relentless

In the pursuit of winning, on and off the field.

U Unstoppable

In overcoming personal, academic and athletic challenges.

N Never satisfied

Always reaching beyond good for something greater.

Join us at Tower Club on Friday, May 7, 2027, as these honors are presented.
